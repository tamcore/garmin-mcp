Connect a client:

  1. Register the client's callback URL in oauth.clients. For ChatGPT, add the
     server at https://chatgpt.com/plugins and register
     https://chatgpt.com/connector/oauth/* with
     config.oauthAllowRedirectWildcards=true.
  2. Point the client at {{ .Values.config.publicURL }}.
  3. Each user completes the Garmin login in the browser once, at
     {{ trimSuffix "/mcp" .Values.config.publicURL }}/login.
