This form belongs to this garmin-mcp deployment. It is not hosted by Garmin, and Garmin does not operate it.

What you type here is forwarded to Garmin for this one login, and for nothing else. Neither value is ever written to a log, and neither is ever shown to {{.Client.ClientName}}, which receives only a token issued by this deployment.

Your password is used for this login and then discarded: it is never written to disk. Your email address is stored, so that this deployment recognises your account the next time you sign in. The Garmin tokens this login produces are stored encrypted.
